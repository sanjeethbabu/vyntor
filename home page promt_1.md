Create a **professional, automatic hero-section slideshow** for the VYNTOR Technologies website using the following three slides.

### Design and Theme
- Follow the website’s existing visual theme, using **premium gold accents**, elegant typography, clean spacing, and a modern technology-company aesthetic.
- Maintain a sophisticated, minimal, international brand identity.
- Ensure the slideshow integrates seamlessly with the existing page design without disrupting the layout.

### Slide Content

**Slide 1 — Digital Ideas**

**Heading:** Ideas Deserve to Become Reality.

**Description:** We transform your vision into powerful digital solutions built around your needs.

**Slide 2 — Business Solutions**

**Heading:** Technology That Works for You.

**Description:** Simplify your operations, solve real challenges, and move your business forward with tailored technology.

**Slide 3 — Online Business Growth**

**Heading:** Your Next Chapter Starts Here.

**Description:** Establish your digital presence and unlock new opportunities with modern websites, applications, and intelligent solutions.

### Slideshow Behaviour
1. Automatically transition between all three slides at a professional, comfortable interval of approximately 5–6 seconds.
2. Display three small, elegant navigation dots centered at the bottom of the slideshow to indicate the current slide.
3. Highlight the active dot using the website’s gold accent color; keep inactive dots subtle and unobtrusive.
4. Allow users to click a dot to navigate directly to the corresponding slide.
5. When a user selects a dot manually, update the active indicator and reset the automatic slide timer.
6. Use smooth, refined transitions such as a subtle fade with a slight vertical movement. Avoid flashy or distracting animations.
7. Pause automatic transitions while the user hovers over the slideshow on desktop, where appropriate.
8. Support touch-friendly interaction and responsive layouts on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
9. Respect the user's reduced-motion accessibility preference.

### Typography and Layout
- Use a clear visual hierarchy between the heading and description.
- Keep headings prominent, bold, and easy to read.
- Keep descriptions concise, with comfortable line spacing and a controlled maximum width.
- Position the navigation dots consistently at the bottom of the slideshow, with sufficient spacing from the content.
- Maintain consistent alignment, padding, and dimensions across all slides to prevent layout shifts.

### Implementation Requirements
- Inspect the existing website structure, styling, and technology stack before making changes.
- Reuse the existing brand colors, typography, and design components wherever possible.
- Implement reusable, maintainable components with clean code.
- Ensure the slideshow works correctly without layout flickering, overlapping text, or unnecessary re-renders.
- Preserve all existing website functionality and content outside the slideshow.
- Do not redesign the entire page or introduce unrelated elements.

**Final goal:** Deliver an elegant, premium, gold-accented hero slideshow that feels intentionally designed for VYNTOR Technologies—not like a generic carousel. Prioritize visual polish, readability, smooth animation, accessibility, and a consistent brand experience.
